1. Read the ground

Contours — ground shapeLines showing elevation. Tight lines = steeper ground; broad spacing = flatter ground. Useful for tracing gullies, ridges and benches.
10 m terrain relief — broad landformStatewide elevation terrain from the public Vicmap 10 m DEM. Use this for regional landform; it is not 1 m LiDAR.
Water & drainage — where water movesMapped watercourses. Useful alongside terrain for following drainage and alluvial settings.
1 m LiDAR availability — high-resolution coverageShows where Victorian 1 m DEM/LiDAR products exist or are referenced. It is a coverage indicator, not the 1 m surface itself.

2. Find the old gold evidence

Historic mines & mineral sites — old workingsMapped mineral sites and mine locations. Useful for finding previously worked ground and historical mining districts.
Deep leads — buried ancient channelsMapped deep-lead features. These are geological/historical clues, not a claim that gold is present at every point.
Goldfields — historic district extentBroad goldfield polygons. Good for regional context; much less precise than individual workings or geology.

3. Understand the ground

Geology — rock and geological settingBroad geological units. Use it to compare the type of ground around historic workings and goldfields.
Land tenure — access contextLand-tenure context only. Always verify current rules, permissions, closures and fossicking/mining restrictions before entering land.
How the layers differ:
Contours / relief = what the land looks like.
Water = where drainage runs.
LiDAR coverage = where higher-resolution elevation data may be available.
Mines / deep leads = where historical mining evidence is recorded.
Goldfields = broad historic district context.
Geology = what the ground is made of.
Tenure = who/what controls the land.
Important LiDAR note: Victoria's 1 m DEM is LiDAR-derived but statewide public 1 m web access is restricted. This app therefore does not pretend a statewide 1 m LiDAR surface is available. The public statewide 10 m DEM is used for broad terrain, with 1 m coverage treated separately.
Targets are screening areas based on historical/geospatial evidence, not a guarantee of gold. Verify current access, closures, fossicking rules, cultural heritage requirements and safety.
